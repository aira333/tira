Delete your Tira account

Tira is published by UsTechBridge. You can delete your account and everything stored with it at any time.

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Tira and make sure you're signed in.
  2. Tap the Settings tab.
  3. Under Account, tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and all of its data are deleted right away.

Option 2: by email

Email ustechbridge@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Tira account", from the Google email address you sign in to Tira with. We confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your sign-in and Google profile details (name, email, photo link)
  • Appointments and reminders
  • Shopping lists and to-do lists
  • Your saved name, list preferences, ride service and phone numbers

Nothing is kept after deletion. Reminders already scheduled on a phone are cancelled when you delete from the app; uninstalling Tira removes anything left on that phone.

Using Tira without an account?

Then nothing is stored with us. Your data is only on your phone; remove it with Settings → Erase all data, or by uninstalling the app.
