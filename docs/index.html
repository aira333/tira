Your voice assistant for the day ahead.

Say "Tira" and ask in your own words. Tira keeps your appointments, reminders, shopping lists and to-dos, and reads every answer aloud. It's built for people who'd rather talk than tap.

What Tira does

Appointments

Add, find, reschedule and delete by voice, with reminders 15 minutes, 1 hour or 1 day before.

Your day, aloud

"What's my day?" reads today's appointments, open to-dos and your shopping in one go.

Lists

Shopping lists for every store and to-do lists for every project. Check items off by voice or tap.

Rides

Save your transport service once; Tira confirms the details and opens the dialer for you.

Accessible

Large, high-contrast text designed for low vision, screen-reader labels, and a hands-free mode.

Private

No ads or tracking. Use it without an account, or sign in with Google to keep your data on any phone.

Try saying
  • "Tira, add an appointment with Dr. Patel tomorrow at 2 PM"
  • "Add milk and eggs to my Costco shopping list"
  • "What's next?"
