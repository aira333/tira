Privacy Policy

Effective October 22, 2026 · Published by UsTechBridge

In short
  • You can use Tira without an account. Your information then stays on your phone.
  • If you sign in with Google, your Tira data is stored in your private account so it works on any phone.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics.
  • You can delete your account and all of its data at any time from the app.

Who we are

Tira is a voice assistant for appointments, reminders, shopping lists, to-dos and ride requests, published on Google Play by UsTechBridge ("we"). Contact: ustechbridge@gmail.com.

Using Tira without an account

If you choose "Continue without an account", everything Tira saves stays in the app's storage on your phone. We never receive it. Uninstalling the app, or choosing Settings → Erase all data, removes it.

Signing in with Google

If you sign in, your Tira data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, so it is available on any phone where you sign in. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Access rules in the database mean each account can only read and change its own data. Google sign-in is provided by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; Tira only receives your basic profile (name, email, photo).

Calls, reminders and permissions

  • Microphone and speech recognition: used only while Tira is listening, after you tap the mic or turn on hands-free mode. Hands-free mode listens only while the app is open on screen.
  • Notifications: appointment reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • Phone calls: for rides, Tira opens your phone's dialer with your ride service's number. You place the call; Tira does not make, record or listen to calls.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with advertisers or data brokers. We only use the service providers above (Supabase for storage, Google for sign-in and speech recognition) to run the app. We would disclose information only if required by law.

Keeping and deleting your data

Account data is kept until you delete it. To delete your account and all of its data, open Tira → Settings → Delete account; deletion is immediate. You can also email us; see how to delete your account. We complete email requests within 30 days.

Children

Tira is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Significant changes will be shown in the app.
